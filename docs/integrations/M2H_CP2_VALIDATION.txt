TRUTH / IDENTITY
  QBO Invoice maps only to T1. QBO Payment and CreditMemo invoice allocations
  map only to T4. providerAccountId is the connection-scoped QBO realm/company.
  Xero ACCREC Invoice maps only to T1. Payment, CreditNote, Prepayment and
  Overpayment allocations map only to T4. providerAccountId is the Xero
  tenant/organisation, never the OAuth connection ID.
  No accounting Payment establishes T3. No adapter establishes T2, T5 or T6.
  Xero IsReconciled is preserved as provider context and remains insufficient
  to establish DueWatch T6.

CHANGE DETECTION / SYNC
  Refetch obligations retain tenantId + provider + providerAccountId from the
  trusted connection. Current versioned QBO CloudEvents are parsed as separate
  entity/operation/version fields; the old envelope is LEGACY_REPLAY_ONLY.
  Xero's standard events envelope is validated atomically across every event.
  Events never write financial truth. Page replay is provider-
  account scoped, duplicate-idempotent, newest-version preserving, explicitly
  terminal, and fail-closed on failed/incomplete pages.
