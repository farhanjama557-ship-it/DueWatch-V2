TRUTH / IDENTITY
  QBO Invoice maps only to T1. QBO Payment and CreditMemo invoice allocations
  map only to T4. providerAccountId is the connection-scoped QBO realm/company.
  Xero ACCREC Invoice maps only to T1. Payment, CreditNote, Prepayment and
  Overpayment allocations map only to T4. providerAccountId is the Xero
  tenant/organisation, never the OAuth connection ID.
  No accounting Payment establishes T3. No adapter establishes T2, T5 or T6.
  Xero IsReconciled is preserved as provider context and remains insufficient
  to establish DueWatch T6.
