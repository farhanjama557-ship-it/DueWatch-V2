4. COMPANY BRAIN, PRIORITIES, AND CHARACTER
==================================================

buildAskDwCompanyBrainContext projects founder-confirmed understanding,
corrections, conflicts, roles, review/source-change state, and current G5
authority into a separate immutable non-canonical context. It rejects tenant
mismatch, canonical-money/mutable-authority fields, and non-current grants.
Authority descriptions preserve action, scope, channel, approval, limits,
conditions, and effective window. Company Brain cannot override ledger truth.

buildAskDwDailyPriorities has a fixed inspectable order: founder decision,
unresolved conflict, revoked support, changed since review, missing authority,
founder answer, then review. A degraded read is never called a quiet portfolio.

askDwCharacterSpec is the shared answer-first voice contract. Examples cover
greetings, priorities, referents, pressure, evidence, company vocabulary,
authority, client-level answers, Deep alternatives, and acknowledgements using
placeholders only. UI evidence/checks are progressively disclosed. Models cite
only admitted tool-run IDs. Observable structured work is shown; raw private
chain-of-thought is not requested or exposed.
