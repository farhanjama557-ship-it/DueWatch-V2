==================================================
6. COMPANY BRAIN PACKET CONTRACT
==================================================

The Company Brain context is a SEPARATE typed context from caseContext, by
design. caseContext deliberately excludes canonical financial facts and
authority so a conversational reference can never carry either; stuffing
Company Brain into it would quietly break that split. The new context keeps its
own boundary and is inspected before it can reach a model: a canonical-money
field (balance, amount_paid, due_date, canonicalFacts, arState) or a mutable
authority field raises rather than being stripped silently (G7-C4).

Its boundaries are all false except readOnly, asserted field-by-field (G7-C6):
isCanonicalFinancialTruth, canMutateCompanyBrain, canGrantAuthority,
canRevokeAuthority, canExecute, conversationCanMutate,
understandingApprovalIsAuthority, humanRoleIsDwAuthority,
repeatedApprovalIsAuthority, modelConfidenceIsAuthority,
providerCapabilityIsAuthority, usualBehaviourIsPolicy.

A failed Company Brain read reports available:false with reason
COMPANY_BRAIN_READ_UNAVAILABLE. It never answers as though the Company Brain
were empty — those are different answers and only one is honest (G7-C8), and
asserting Company Brain content while that read failed is blocked (G7-G7).

==================================================
7. CONVERSATION BEHAVIOUR
==================================================

Turn types: GREETING, ACKNOWLEDGEMENT, DAILY_PRIORITIES, PORTFOLIO_STATUS,
NEEDS_FOUNDER, WHAT_CHANGED, FOLLOW_UP, EVIDENCE_REQUEST, CORRECTION,
CHALLENGE, COMPANY_BRAIN_QUESTION, AUTHORITY_QUESTION, AR_JOB.

Corrections are split into REFERENT / NEW_EVIDENCE / CHALLENGE because they are
not the same thing. A challenge is pressure; an assertion in chat is flagged
requiresEvidencePath and is not admitted evidence.

"What should I do today?" is answered from buildAskDwDailyPriorities, which
composes the canonical needs-you projection, the G6 read model and the G5
authority projection into a fixed, reviewable order:
  FOUNDER_DECISION_REQUIRED > UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT > SUPPORTING_SOURCE_REVOKED >
  CHANGED_SINCE_REVIEW > BLOCKED_ON_MISSING_AUTHORITY > NEEDS_FOUNDER_ANSWER >
  AWAITING_REVIEW
Every entry carries the exact reason it ranked, so "why is this first?" has a
real answer. The model does not choose the order (boundaries.modelChoseOrder is
false). A degraded read is never smoothed into "nothing needs you": that
sentence is only sayable when everything deciding it was readable (G7-P3).

==================================================
9. CHARACTER, GROUNDING AND SYCOPHANCY
==================================================

askDwCharacterSpec.js is the single definition of DW's voice, so runtime and
tests cannot drift. Filler is measured as a RATE, not banned outright: one
warranted "in conclusion" is not a regression, a persona built from filler is
(G7-K5). Sycophantic reversal language and repeated openers are detected.

enforceAskDwGrounding runs after the existing citation check and only ever
downgrades a verdict (G7-G10). It blocks, with no model involved:
  invented amounts and identifiers        UNGROUNDED_AMOUNT / _IDENTIFIER
  any claim an action was performed       CLAIMED_EXECUTION
  authority claimed with no grant         CLAIMED_AUTHORITY_WITHOUT_GRANT
  "they paid" without canonical support   UNSUPPORTED_PAYMENT_CLAIM
  prose deciding an undecided conflict    RESOLVED_AN_UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT
  Company Brain claims while read down    COMPANY_BRAIN_CLAIM_UNAVAILABLE
  reversal under pressure, no evidence    SYCOPHANTIC_REVERSAL

Sycophancy: tone may adapt, facts may not. Ten consecutive pushes do not soften
the guard (G7-G9), and four separate pushes inside the payment-pressure
conversation are each still blocked (G7-B7).
